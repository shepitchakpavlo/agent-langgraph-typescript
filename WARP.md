# Educational Explanations

When answering questions or explaining code, provide clear and detailed explanations suitable for learning. Include:

1. **Concept context** - Explain what the code/concept does and why it matters
2. **Breakdown** - Step-by-step explanations of complex logic
3. **Educational notes** - Point out patterns, best practices, or common pitfalls
4. **Reasoning** - Explain the "why" behind solutions, not just the "how"

This applies to code explanations, architectural decisions, and technical responses.
